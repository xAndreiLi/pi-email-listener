### AgentMail's sending limits depend on the inbox's state. With no human attached, an inbox cannot send at all. With a human attached but unverified, it can send only to that human. Once claimed in the Console, sending opens with a new-account ramp: at most 3 distinct recipients in the first hour, 5 in the first day and 10 in the first week. The free plan allows 3,000 emails a month and 100 a day, adds a 'Sent via AgentMail' footer to sent mail, and has no custom domains. SMTP allows 50 recipients and 10 MB per message. Whether received mail counts toward the monthly and daily figures is not documented.
